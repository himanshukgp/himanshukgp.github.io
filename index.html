About

I'm a senior software engineer at PayGlocal in Bengaluru, where I work on the plumbing behind card payments: mirroring escrow accounts across bank integrations, matching every internal event against bank statements, and making sure disputes don't fall through the cracks.

Before that I was the founding engineer at an EdTech startup, shipping an AI learning platform and a WhatsApp bot on my own. Earlier I worked on supply planning at Flipkart. I studied Mathematics and Computing at IIT Kharagpur, where I also published research on EEG signal classification.

I care most about idempotency, ordering and auditability: the properties that decide whether a financial system can be trusted.
